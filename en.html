Privacy policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

Ondu is a diary for curly hair: you log washes, the products you use and hairdresser visits, and you test products with experiments. This page explains what data the app uses, where it goes and how to delete it. The Italian version is the reference text.

Who is responsible

Ramona Ruoppo, who develops Ondu as an individual. For any privacy question: ramonaruoppo.rr@gmail.com.

In short

  • No ads, no profiling, no usage analytics: we don't sell or share your data.
  • Your data lives on your phone first. A copy goes to the cloud (Google Firebase, servers in Europe) so you don't lose it and can find it on another phone.
  • Original photos stay on the phone: only a thumbnail goes to the cloud, without location or other hidden data.
  • You can export and delete everything from the app, whenever you want.

Legal basis

We use the data to provide the service you asked for by opening and using the app (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). Signing in with Google is optional.

Where it goes and who processes it

  • On your phone, in the app's storage. It may be included in your phone backup (iCloud or Google) if enabled.
  • Google Firebase (Google Ireland Limited), which provides the database (Cloud Firestore, servers in the European Union) and accounts (Firebase Authentication). Google processes the data on our behalf. Some account data may be processed outside the EU, with the safeguards required by the GDPR (standard contractual clauses and the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework). Firebase privacy.
  • Firebase Crashlytics (Google), when the app closes because of an error: it receives the report described above and keeps it for 90 days. Firebase privacy.
  • Open Beauty Facts: when you scan a barcode, the app asks this open database for the product name. It only receives the barcode (and, like any website, your IP address), not who you are. Open Beauty Facts privacy.

The wash reminder is a notification scheduled on the phone: it doesn't go through any server.

How long

As long as you use the app. If you delete a wash, photo or product, it's removed from the cloud too. "Delete my data" or "Delete account" erases everything right away; offline, the cloud part completes as soon as the phone is back online.

Your rights

You can access, correct, delete and port your data, object to or restrict processing. Most of it you can do in the app:

  • Profile → Export my data: a file with your diary, products and experiments.
  • Profile → Delete my data or Delete account: how it works.

For anything else, email ramonaruoppo.rr@gmail.com. You can also complain to your data protection authority (in Italy, the Garante).

Children

Ondu is not meant for anyone under 14.

Security

Each account can only read and write its own cloud space. Data is encrypted in transit between the phone and the servers.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published here with the date at the top updated.
